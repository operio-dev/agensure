The Problem

Why standard prompts and static guardrails
fail under pressure.

Your tech team configures system prompts and basic filters. But LLMs are probabilistic, non-deterministic engines. Standard barriers are structurally fragile under adversarial attacks.

1

The Article 50 Liability Trap

If your agent cedes to an adversarial jailbreak and mimics human behavior or fails mandatory disclosures, it directly violates the EU AI Act starting August 2, 2026. Non-compliance exposes your company to sanzioni up to €15M or 3% of global turnover.

2

Direct Financial Exposure

An automated prompt injection attack can hijack your customer-facing bot, forcing it to authorize fake transactions, pricing overrides, or unapproved refunds that legally commit your company. Traditional cyber insurance explicitly excludes these autonomous software liabilities today.

3

The Data Drift Vulnerability

Relying on snapshot security evaluations creates a lethal gap. AI risk is dynamic. Silent model updates from foundation providers can suddenly alter behavioral safety bounds without your developers knowing, leaving inputs and PII exposed.

“If your conversational agent causes a financial or regulatory breach, you - as the deploying company - are 100% liable. Traditional cyber insurance won't cover you.”

One Proprietary Risk Score

The Agensure Risk Score (ARS)
Framework

A single, predictive number from 1 to 100. Calibrated on the 70/30 engineering rule.

ARS1 to 100.
01

The 70/30 Risk Split

We formalize LLM physics: 70% of the vulnerability is intrinsic to the base model (mapped in our laboratory), while 30% represents the delta introduced by your specific system prompt. We test the text of your prompt against our baseline to calculate an immediate, mathematically sound risk profile.

02

Behavioral Failure Rate

Our adversarial suite attacks your endpoint across five distinct risk domains. We measure exactly how many probes force the model to drift, leak data, or break role rules. Every failure is reproducible and verifiable in your logs.

03

Financial Blast Radius

We factor in the operational authority your bot holds. A standard customer support chatbot and an agent connected to transaction APIs carry identical 70% model baselines, but carry vastly different financial blast radii. We calculate the score accordingly.

04

Security Transparency

The exact probe vectors and their deployment sequence remain proprietary to prevent adversarial exploitation. Much like Moody's or S&P, we publish the rigorous methodological framework and the resulting score, protecting the security of your system.
